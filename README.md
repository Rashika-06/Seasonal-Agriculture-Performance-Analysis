# Seasonal-Agriculture-Performance-Analysis
Data visualization and analysis of seasonal agricultural performance using Python.

## 📌 Project Overview

Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis is a data visualization project that analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons.

The project uses a dataset containing **4,000 farm records** with information about crops, seasons, weather conditions, soil conditions, irrigation methods, yield, production, cost, revenue, profit, and water usage.

The main aim of this project is to identify useful patterns and relationships in agricultural data and provide data-driven insights for better agricultural planning.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To analyze agricultural performance across different seasons and identify patterns that can help improve:

- Crop yield
- Agricultural profitability
- Production
- Water efficiency
- Resource management

---

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

- Checked the structure and dimensions of the dataset.
- Identified missing values.
- Handled missing values using median values.
- Checked for duplicate records.
- No duplicate records were found.
- Explored categorical variables such as season, crop, and irrigation method.

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project analyzes:

1. Average crop yield by season
2. Average crop yield by crop
3. Crop performance across different seasons
4. Average profit by crop
5. Average profit by season
6. Rainfall vs crop yield
7. Fertilizer usage vs crop yield
8. Soil moisture vs crop yield
9. Disease and pest risk by season
10. Irrigation method vs crop yield
11. Water efficiency by irrigation method
12. Agricultural production by season
13. Market price vs profit
14. Cost and revenue comparison by season

---

## 📈 Key Findings

- **Kharif** recorded the highest average crop yield among the three seasons.
- **Sugarcane** recorded the highest average crop yield among the crops analyzed.
- Sugarcane also recorded the highest average profit in the dataset.
- Crop performance varies across different seasons.
- Irrigation methods show differences in crop yield and water efficiency.
- The relationship between rainfall and crop yield was found to be weak in this dataset.
